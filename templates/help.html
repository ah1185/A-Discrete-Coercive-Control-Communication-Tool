About

Modern technological advancements have undoubtedly transformed interpersonal relationships and have prompted widespread reconsideration of everyday challenges regarding privacy, constant connectivity, and surveillance. One advancement in particular that encourages this connectivity is the Internet of Things (IoT), a network of 'smart' devices such as vehicles, doorbells, or other physical objects capable of sharing data. While such an integration offers undeniable convenience, it also expands how those with malicious intent can inflict harm on others. This project aims to develop a system for victims to communicate with their support network during potentially dangerous situations, whilst leveraging the IoT to deliver messages discreetly, avoiding detection from an abuser.
